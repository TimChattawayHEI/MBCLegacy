# Orpington 2001: design notes

A group night out. Meet at the bus stop by Pizza Express, over the mini roundabout, past Ram Ba Ba, into the Blacksmiths, back to Ram Ba Ba for the coach, and on to Atlantis in Croydon. At 17, getting into Ram Ba Ba got you on the Atlantis coach with no ID check at the club. Kelly upset the bouncers once and sat on the coach till 2am while everyone else went in.

## The real street (left to right)

1. Pizza Express corner: three storeys, mansard roof with dormers, white rendered corner bay over the door, blue PIZZA EXPRESS roundel, bus stop. To its left a TO LET board, barbers and bakeries; opposite, green-fronted shops and an orange Belisha beacon.
2. The mini roundabout: painted white circle and arrows, zebra crossing, Belisha beacons.
3. Ram Ba Ba: same mansard style in darker brick on the corner of Church Hill (white side door, CHURCH HILL sign). Young's fascia, RAM BA BA and CAFE BAR lettering, white window frames with X-pattern rails, roller shutter on the corner door, a 2nd floor TO LET board, the IB 798 bollard.
4. A small car park (later Aksular's).
5. The Blacksmiths' single storey wing: white painted brick, black plinth, flower planters along the roof.
6. Ye Olde Blacksmith: white two storeys, black louvred shutters, black fascia with gold gothic lettering, the anvil and smith bracket sign over the door, brass lanterns, hanging baskets, barrel planters, chalkboards, flagpole. No bunting for 2001 (the St George bunting in the photo is the 2002 World Cup). Easter egg: a faded ARTICHOKE INN ghost sign (it was the Artichoke in 1982).
7. The mirror and lamp shop: low tiled roof, two weatherboarded dormers, shopfront full of mirrors and lamps, and PRIVATE PROPERTY! NO PARKING painted on the brick gable.

## Inside the Blacksmiths

L-shaped room. Come in through the front door; the bar fills the corner that turns the square into an L (on the right as you enter). Pool table at the top of the L. Old blacksmith's tools, wagon wheels and the like on the walls, fruit machines dotted about, seating round the edges.

Run by Mick and Sue (East End cockney). Mick: big, fat, bald, old. Sue: 2000s older landlady, short blonde going grey. Their sons: Alan (thin, blonde, unkempt, always half-cut) and Jeff (huge, short dark hair, dirty polo shirts). Use lightly tweaked names in game.

## Atlantis, Croydon

Gold ATLANTIS on a navy oval with a merman crest, NIGHTCLUB in blue under it, white wave-shaped steel canopy over the entrance, yellow brick with brown bands, white panelling above, blue bollards, posters in ornate gold frames. DJ EZ on the posters and as a silhouette in the booth only.
