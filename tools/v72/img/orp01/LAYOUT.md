# Orpington 2001 panels (raw Meshy, 1376x768)

Street strip (street_strip.webp, 5408x768) is the panels pasted in this order at these raw x positions, later ones on top:
p0@0, p1@1032, p2@2064, b2@1720, p3n@3000, b3@2332, p4@4032, b4@3688. Then the second and third moons are painted out
(sky filled smoothly between median edge colours over x 1380-1590 y 0-175 and x 4100-4210 y 0-120, with a few stars).

- p0: Pizza Express corner and bus stop. p1: the mini roundabout and the Ram Ba Ba corner. p2: car park (its right end, an angled pub, is covered).
- b2: bridge, Ram Ba Ba into the car park. b3: bridge, car park into the Blacksmiths wing. b4: bridge, the pub into the mirror shop.
- p3n: Ye Olde Blacksmith, wing and front, flat on, ARTICHOKE INN ghost sign. p4: the mirror shop and the NO PARKING gable.

Blacksmiths inside (pub_strip.webp): bi0@0 (front door, seating, fruit machines), bi1@1032 (the bar in the corner of the L, bell over it), bi2@2064 (pool room, fire exit).

Atlantis: at0 (outside, DJ EZ posters, NO TRAINERS), ai0 (inside, DJ EZ banner and booth silhouette).
