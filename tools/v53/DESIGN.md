# Nightlife 2006: design

A new chapter between Elba 2002 and Carp Diem: a Friday night out in London in 2006, from Orpington station to the
N47 home. Five stages, WhatsApp chats between them (2006 era: Sony Ericsson-style phone screens, not iPhones).

## Decisions (Tim, 18 Oct)
- Enemy groups are designed by look and attitude, not race; every group is a mix. No "pikey" label.
- The watch crew is a named crew of watch thieves with no nationality on screen.
- No homeless enemies. A homeless bloke on the N47 is on your side and lobs you a weapon.
- The chapter is part of the full campaign, in date order (1999, Elba 2002, Nightlife 2006, Carp Diem).

## Chats
- Opening: the lads organise the night, meet at Orpington station at 8. Sam: "left already, 5 mins". 30 minutes later he's still "5 mins".
- After Charing Cross: good night so far... has anyone seen Seb? Someone saw him round the back by the smoking area.
- After the smoking area: Seb's watch is back. Missed the last train. N47 it is. Marc won't pay the bus fare: he's walking home (it's a four-hour walk).
- Ending: home; Marc still walking at sunrise.

## Stage 1: Orpington Station
- Station Approach, the ticket hall, the barriers, the footbridge, platform 2. Late Friday, orange sodium lights.
- Enemies: local lads and lasses (existing hoodie/tracksuit/townie types), a couple of early drunks.
- Mini-boss at the barriers: THE TICKET INSPECTOR ("Tickets, please."). Moves: ticket-machine swipe, clipboard slap,
  blocks with the barrier gate, calls "PENALTY FARE!" and fines you (knocks your score back unless you hit him first).
- Ends boarding the train.

## Stage 2: The 21:12 to Charing Cross
- Inside a 2006 Networker: blue moquette 3+2 seats, yellow grab poles, luggage racks, sliding doors, dark windows
  with the suburbs streaming past. The whole carriage rumbles and sways; tighter shakes over points.
- You walk carriage to carriage towards the front. Between carriages: connecting doors. Every few carriages a toilet:
  OUT OF ORDER, OUT OF ORDER, OUT OF ORDER... the last one slides open on a bloke mid-business ("OI! SHUT THE DOOR!").
- Announcer: "The next station is... Petts Wood / Chislehurst / Elmstead Woods / Grove Park / Hither Green / Lewisham /
  London Bridge / Waterloo East / Charing Cross, where this train terminates." "Mind the gap."
- At each stop the train brakes (everyone lurches), the doors open, the window shows the platform, that stop's lot
  get on, scared passengers run back down the train past you.
  - Chislehurst: City Boy, It Girl (posh, expensive, not cartoonish).
  - Hither Green: Burberry Cap, Hoops.
  - Lewisham: the snood gang (Snood, Puffer, Hood: black hoodies and snoods, mixed faces). Your lad: "Lewisham.
    Dangerous. Still, could be worse. Could be Croydon."
- Mid-boss after Lewisham: LEWISHAM LENNY. Train mechanics: pulls the passenger alarm (emergency brake: everyone
  slides down the carriage, him included, then he charges), swings off the grab poles into a two-footed kick, rips a
  door open while the train's moving (wind blows everyone towards it; don't get pushed near it), throws luggage off
  the racks.

## Stage 3: Charing Cross
- Off the train, platform to concourse (departure boards, M&S Simply Food, Burger King), through the ticket barriers.
- Enemies: leftovers from the train plus the Friday office-party crowd.
- Ends at the barriers: cinematic camera out onto Trafalgar Square at night (Nelson's Column, the lions, fountains,
  the National Gallery). "London. 22:47."

## Stage 4: The Smoking Area
- The back door of a bar off the Strand: brick alley, fire door, smoking shelter, bins, kegs, fairy lights.
- If you're Seb: cutscene, Seb on the back step with the crew round him chatting him up while one works his watch off.
  He clocks it. RAGE: just for this stage, Seb glows red, hits harder, shrugs off grabs; it builds as he fights.
- Anyone else: you come out the fire door to find Seb mid-situation; he realises, the crew turn on you, and Seb fights
  beside you (an ally lad, like Tim's helpers).
- The watch crew (named crew, no nationality):
  - THE GRAPPLER: bear-hugs you from behind and holds you; mash to break free. While you're held, others go for you.
  - THE DIPPER: slim, quick; sidesteps your punches, lifts your weapon or a slice of your score ("NICKED!"), runs off.
  - THE LOOKOUT: hangs back, whistles more in, lobs bottles.
- Boss: THE JEWELLER (watches up both arms, gold chains). Moves: blinding watch-glint flash (look away: stand still to
  avoid), "what time is it?" feint then counter-grab, chain whip. Beat him: Seb gets his watch back (cutscene).

## Stage 5: The N47
- Top deck of a night bus, moving: London at night through the windows (kebab shops, chicken shops, bus stops,
  Lewisham clock tower). The bus brakes and corners: everyone gets thrown left or right.
- Hold the top deck: waves come up the stairs at the back. Survive to the stop (a progress strip of stops).
- Smash the seat-back grab poles and the window hammers for weapons; the "emergency exit" window can throw enemies out.
- Enemies: a stag do (the stag in a banana costume, groomsmen in matching "DAVE'S LAST NIGHT OF FREEDOM" t-shirts),
  kebab-shop stragglers, leftovers from the train and the crew.
- Friendly: a homeless bloke asleep on the back seat wakes up, says something kind and chucks you a weapon (once or
  twice during the ride).
- Marc isn't on the bus (he's walking home) unless you're playing as him; then he's paid after all ("fine. fine.").

## Art (PixelLab)
- Characters (v3, side view, 64px; bosses 80px): City Boy, It Girl, Burberry Cap, Hoops, Snood, Puffer, Hood,
  Ticket Inspector, Lewisham Lenny, Grappler, Dipper, Lookout, The Jeweller, The Stag, Groomsman, the friendly
  homeless bloke, two fleeing passengers. Rows: idle, walk, attack, hurt, knockdown (+ boss extras).
- Backgrounds: Orpington station ticket hall and platform, train carriage interior (repeating), suburbs at night
  through the windows, platform through the windows, Charing Cross concourse, Trafalgar Square at night, the back
  alley, N47 top deck interior, London high street at night through the bus windows.
- Props: ticket barriers, departure board, connecting door, toilet door, luggage, bins/kegs (existing), seat poles.

## Music (engine.py)
- 2006 sounds: electro house (station), bassline / niche (train), funky house (Charing Cross), grime (smoking area),
  and a night-bus track; one boss theme for the chapter.
