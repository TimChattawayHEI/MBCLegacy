# MBC Legacies

The lads vs everybody. A 16-bit beat 'em up, Orpington 1999 to Elba 2002, a night out in London in 2006, then Denise's Fishery.

## Get the game (Android)

**[Download the latest APK](https://github.com/TimChattawayHEI/MBCLegacy/releases/latest/download/MBC-Legacies.apk)**

Open the file on your phone. The first time, Android asks whether to allow installs from your browser or files app: allow it. To update, install the new one over the top.

## How to play

Move with the stick (hold it right out to run), HIT to attack (tap it for your combo), JUMP, and SPECIAL when the bar is full. Hold HIT and let go for your lad's mini special: it hits everyone around you, but costs a little health if it lands. PLAY ONLINE on the select screen starts a two-player game with a friend's phone: one hosts and reads out the code, the other joins.

## What's here

- `web/` the game itself (one page plus its art, music and fonts). The same game runs on claude.ai.
- `android/` a small Android app that shows the game full screen.
- `tools/title3d_build.py` wires the 3D title and select screens (`tools/title3d/`) and any-stage starts into `web/index.html`. Run it again after rebuilding the page from older sources.
- `tools/v44_build.py` (run after `title3d_build.py`) is the Version 44 optimisation pass: no full-size copies of the sprite sheets, skies and the fishery car park drawn once and reused, all art preloaded with a LOADING wait at stage start, and the old vector fallback art removed. Run it again after rebuilding the page from claude.ai.
- `tools/v45_build.py` (run after `v44_build.py`) is the Version 45 quality of life pass: CARRY ON a run after the app closes, RESTART STAGE and a SETTINGS panel in the pause menu (music and effects volume, vibration, gentler shake and flashes, touch button size, see-through buttons, left-handed stick), a SKIP button on group chats, and phone vibration.
- `tools/v47_build.py` (run after `v45_build.py`): hold the touch stick right out to run (a ring fills round the knob first), plus a RUNNING setting for running on any full push. Every lad has his own select-screen pose in `web/sprites/pose_<id>.png` (Version 48): a PixelLab animation drawn three-quarter on (south-east), as a strip of frames at the sheet's cell size with the feet on the sheet's anchor. Frame 0 shows while he's chosen; the rest play when he's picked.
- `tools/v49_build.py` (run after `v47_build.py`): boss finishes (freeze, flash, music cut, slow motion and a punch-in on the last boss of a stage), boss intro cards that take the place of the old boss banners, and S to D stage ranks on the points screen, with the best kept per stage (`carpdiem.ranks`) and shown on the 3D stage list.
- `tools/v50_build.py` (run after `v49_build.py`): Thet, the unlockable eighth lad. His sheet `web/sprites/thet.png` and `tools/v50/thet_meta.json` come from `tools/v50/thet_sheet.py`, which lines up the PixelLab workbench sheets in `tools/v50/src/`. Also edits `tools/title3d/title3d.js` so the 3D select keeps him.
- `tools/v51_build.py` (run after `v50_build.py`): new hand grip points for the weapons that were redrawn in PixelLab (the PNGs in `web/bg/weapons` and `web/bg/shots` are the PixelLab versions).
- `tools/v52_build.py` (run after `v51_build.py`): draws the last code-drawn game objects from PixelLab images in `web/bg/fx` (Master Control's transmitter, the Parkie's litter picker, the far bank's spod, boilies and float, Big Gary's and Big Gaz's waves, the moon, clouds and fireworks) and Big Gaz's small head flames from the PixelLab fire effect.
- `tools/v53_build.py` (run after `v52_build.py`; it also edits `tools/title3d/title3d.js` and `.css`): the Nightlife 2006 chapter, five stages between Elba and Carp Diem. The chapter's code is `tools/v53/nightlife.js`, injected whole between `// NL js start` and `// NL js end`. Art: `tools/v53/foe_sheets.py` lines up the PixelLab workbench sheets in `tools/v53/src/` into `web/sprites/<key>.png` plus `tools/v53/sheets.json` (layout and frame picks); `tools/v53/bg_process.py` turns the raw images in `tools/v53/img/` into `web/bg/nightlife/` (repainting garbled signs with `pxfont.py`, cutting the train and bus windows into glass); `tools/v53/seams.py` makes the canvases for the inpainted joins that let the station, Charing Cross and alley backgrounds run on without a cut and the bus pole prop and weapon. Design notes: `tools/v53/DESIGN.md`, `ART_BRIEF.md`, `ART_DONE.md`, `pixellab_ids.md`. Music sources: `tools/v53/music/`.
- `tools/v54_build.py` (run after `v53_build.py`; it also edits `tools/title3d/title3d.js`): Ash, the second unlockable lad, a head taller than everyone, and a kickboxer: he never punches (his jab and hook rows are a low kick and a roundhouse; weapons still swing). Special THE ASHINATOR (he turns into the Ashinator, fires nine shots, all of them into the boss if there is one, then walks off: he'll be back), mini special GINGER SNAP. Unlocked by finishing the campaign on Hard, typing ASH on the select screen, or ASHY as an online join code. His sheets `web/sprites/ash.png`, `ashn.png` (the Ashinator) and `pose_ash.png`, and `tools/v54/ash_meta.json`, come from `tools/v54/ash_sheet.py`, which lines up the PixelLab workbench sheets in `tools/v54/src/`.
- `tools/v55_build.py` (run after `v54_build.py`; it also edits `tools/title3d/title3d.html` and `.js` and re-runs `title3d_build.py`): THE HARVEST MOON (the pub; Version 55 called it The Local). Points are the currency: every stage banks what it earned onto your pub tab. The pub sits between chapters on a run (offline) and is open from the select screen (THE PUB, or N). Inside: the bar (a round on a run is an extra life; a pint from the menu makes the darts wobble), nine-dart darts, a fruit machine with holds and nudges (three Marcs is the jackpot), and four arcade cabinets that open once you've met their game on the campaign: PENALTIES (Byron v the Parkie, after The Frequency), KISS CHASE (Seb, New Year's Eve, after Soundz FM), STAY AWAKE (Sam's 4am drive, after The Club) and THE LONG DRIVE (Pearcy at the fishery, after The Arrangement). Typing ARCADE on the select screen opens them all. Every game has an all-time board: a chalkboard by it with the top three and your best, the record holder's face on the cabinet, and the full board on JUMP. The code is `tools/v55/pub.js`, injected whole between `// PUB js start` and `// PUB js end`; the art in `web/bg/pub/` comes from `tools/v55/art.py` (PixelLab images in `tools/v55/img/`, workbench animation sheets in `tools/v55/src/`, and the dartboard drawn from a real board's sizes). `tools/v55/shots.py` plays every game in headless Chromium (the page has a `?pubtest` hook for it). Version 55.2: on a touch screen the pub, darts, Kiss Chase and Stay Awake use the fight's own stick and buttons, and `tools/v55/font_five.py` redraws the pixel font's 5 with a flat top (it looked like an S, so scores and timers read as a backwards 2).
  The shared boards need one Firebase rule (Realtime Database > Rules, next to `rooms`): `"boards": { "$game": { ".read": true, ".indexOn": ["score"], "$id": { ".write": "!data.exists()", ".validate": "newData.hasChildren(['name', 'lad', 'score', 'at']) && newData.child('score').isNumber() && newData.child('name').val().length <= 12" } } }`. Without it, scores stay on each phone.
- `tools/v56_build.py` (run after `v55_build.py`; it re-runs `v55_build.py` and `title3d_build.py` and edits `tools/title3d/`): your name. The first time you reach the select screen you pick a name (letters and numbers, 3 to 12); "You: NAME" on the select screen changes it. In the app it's claimed in Firebase (`players/<NAME>`, with a secret key only that phone holds), so nobody else can take it. Every board, the pub games and the main high scores (now shared through Firebase in the app, `boards/runs`), keeps one row per player: their best, with the lad they used. A run's score saves under your name without asking, and the online lobby shows names. The code is the "Version 56: your name" section of `tools/v55/pub.js`.
  Firebase rules (Realtime Database > Rules), the whole thing: `{ "rules": { ".read": false, ".write": false, "rooms": { "$code": { ".read": true, ".write": true, ".validate": "$code.matches(/^[A-Z]{4}$/)" } }, "players": { "$name": { ".write": "!data.exists() || data.child('key').val() === newData.child('key').val()", ".validate": "$name.matches(/^[A-Z0-9]{3,12}$/) && newData.hasChildren(['key', 'at']) && newData.child('key').isString() && newData.child('key').val().length >= 16", "at": { ".read": true } } }, "boards": { "$game": { ".read": true, ".indexOn": ["score"], "$name": { ".write": "root.child('players').child($name).exists() && (!data.exists() || newData.child('score').val() > data.child('score').val())", ".validate": "newData.hasChildren(['name', 'lad', 'score', 'at']) && newData.child('score').isNumber() && newData.child('name').val() === $name" } } } } }`. Without them, names aren't claimed and scores stay on each phone; everything goes up the next time the app opens once they're in.
- Version 57 (also built by `v56_build.py`; the code is the "Version 57" section of `tools/v55/pub.js`): the Harvest Moon is where the lads meet. Everyone with the app open is online (`online/<NAME>`: where they are, which lad, and in the pub where they're stood, removed when the app closes or goes to the background). In the pub the others walk about live with their names over their heads; anyone not in the pub stands at the bar with what they're up to (picking a lad, in the lobby, playing a stage, on a cabinet, in an online game). Walk up to someone and HIT to invite them: you host an online game and they get a card (JOIN / NOT NOW) over the pub, the select screen or the boards; JOIN drops them into your lobby, NOT NOW tells you. A game that started in the pub ends back in it. The select screen's pub button says how many are online. Test two or three phones on one computer with `?netmock&pubtest` (the hub uses a BroadcastChannel relay there).
  The Firebase rules gain `"online": { ".read": true, "$name": { ".write": "root.child('players').child($name).exists()", ".validate": "newData.hasChildren(['where', 'at'])" } }` and `"invites": { "$to": { ".read": true, "$from": { ".write": "root.child('players').child($to).exists() && root.child('players').child($from).exists()", ".validate": "newData.hasChildren(['code', 'at']) && newData.child('code').isString() && newData.child('code').val().matches(/^[A-Z]{4}$/)" } } }` next to `players` and `boards`.
- Version 58 (built by `v56_build.py`; the "Version 58" section of `tools/v55/pub.js`): chat in the Harvest Moon. A CHAT tab on the right of the pub (with an unread count) slides out a panel: quick lines (Game?, Pint?, Get in!, Back in 5, Night lads, and one in your lad's voice), a box to type your own (140 characters, one message every 1.2 seconds), and the last 50 messages newest first with faces, names and how long ago. Anything said pops up as a speech bubble over the sayer's head in the pub for six seconds. Unread messages show on the select screen's pub button. Firebase `chat/<id>`; the rules gain `"chat": { ".read": true, ".indexOn": ["at"], "$id": { ".write": "!data.exists() && newData.exists() && root.child('players').child(newData.child('name').val()).exists()", ".validate": "newData.hasChildren(['name', 'text', 'at']) && newData.child('text').isString() && newData.child('text').val().length > 0 && newData.child('text').val().length <= 140" } }`.
- `tools/v59_build.py` (re-runs `v56_build.py` first; build the whole chain from a pre-Version-55 page with `git show e61395a:web/index.html > web/index.html && python3 tools/v59_build.py`): KEBAB SIEGE. A horde fight on the real fight engine (`tools/v59/kebab.js`, between `// KB js start` and `// KB js end`): a fixed screen in a kebab shop at 3am (`web/bg/pub/kebab_wall.png`, PixelLab), the 2006 crowd coming in through the door, endless waves that grow and toughen, one life. Ticket 50, they're on 45; each wave cleared calls the next number, and at yours your kebab's up (full health, 5,000) and you get a new ticket. Knock someone down by the door and they're thrown out. Played once on a run after The Smoking Area (the campaign's run is put aside and back exactly as it was, and CARRY ON still points at the campaign), and as the seventh game in the Harvest Moon (1,500 a go). Also fixed here: the pub's mini-game loop no longer shares a name with the lads' mini specials, and `title3d_build.py` no longer puts back an old `chapterOf` without Nightlife.
- `web/vendor/three.module.min.js` is three.js r165 (MIT, see `three.LICENSE`), used by the title screen in the app.
- `.github/workflows/apk.yml` builds the APK on every push to `main` and publishes it as the latest release.

The signing key (`android/mbc.keystore`) is in the repo on purpose, so every build installs over the previous one. This is a game for friends, not a Play Store app.
